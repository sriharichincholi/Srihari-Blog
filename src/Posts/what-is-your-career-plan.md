Hello, I’m Srihari!

I’m an 18-year-old IT student who recently started my undergraduate journey in Information Technology. When I’m not studying, you’ll usually find me playing video games, building (or breaking) software, or practicing music.

I’m writing this post as an official response to a question every neighborhood uncle or relative at a family gathering loves to ask: *"What is your career plan? Where do you want to go next?"*

To give a bit of context: I’ve always had an appetite for STEM. I was exposed to computers at a young age—waiting for new *Angry Birds* updates, *Need for Speed* releases, and trying out new *Pokémon* titles as a typical free-to-play gamer. Naturally, that early curiosity steered me toward engineering.

I was fortunate to remain in the top 8 students at my institution in 10th grade, win STEM Olympiad medals from school to district levels, and clear competitive exams like JEE Main and TG EAPCET, securing a rank of 3,700—a milestone I’m deeply grateful for.

## Why Systems, Games, and Math?

For a long time, my default plan was straightforward: build games. But I kept running into a familiar pattern. I’d start building an engine or real-time mechanic, hit a ridiculously complex technical wall, fight it endlessly, rage-quit, and then inevitably return because the core puzzle was too interesting to leave unsolved.

Over time, I realized I’m not tied to a single traditional title—I’m a free explorer. I like taking on absurd side-quests, diving into far-fetched ideas, and building prototypes even if they aren't completely feasible or polished yet.

That curiosity eventually pulled me into low-latency systems and mathematical modeling.

Initially, I assumed quantitative finance was just corporate stock management. But once I saw high-performance C++ systems engineered to trim execution delays down to microseconds—and even nanoseconds—it clicked instantly. It wasn't about the finance; it was about **pure, unadulterated systems speed and mathematical rigor.**

At my core, I'm a mix of a **GameDev Junkie** and a **Mathematics Geek**:

* **Game Engineering:** Building 3D browser arenas, socket pipelines, particle systems, and real-time state machines using Three.js and Node.js.
* **Low-Latency & Math:** Tinkering with memory locality, matrix algebra, and algorithmic efficiency in raw C++.
* **Side-Quest Mindset:** Building things for the sheer thrill of seeing how code behaves when pushed to its limits.

I don't need a single box to fit into. Whether it’s architecting a web-game multiplayer engine or diving deep into algorithmic systems, I'm going to keep exploring, breaking, and building cool stuff along the way.

Wishing myself the best of luck on the journey.

— **Srihari**
